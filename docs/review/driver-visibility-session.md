# Driver visibility session

Status: **open — no physical visibility claim**.

Test each measured eye point and driver-height preset with the temporary wheel
and display frame. Enter real millimetre measurements into the review input.

- Automated result: attach occlusion JSON, SVG, and screenshots.
- Human observation: assess speed, gear, RPM, critical-warning region, and
  freshness/connection state without moving the head unnaturally.
- Physical measurement: wheel diameter/centre, eye point, display dimensions,
  mounting offset, dashboard edge, and sightline.
- Reviewer decision: accept, adjust mounting/UI safe zone, or reject.
- Open issue: record every possibly occluded critical region.
- Retest requirement: required after mounting, wheel, seat, geometry, scaling,
  font, or critical-region changes.

The calculator reports geometry intersection only; it is not proof of safe
visibility or road suitability.
