# Daylight presentation session

Status: **open — representative lighting and panels required**.

- Automated result: attach theme/brightness state and display profile.
- Human observation: assess readability, reflections, washout, warning
  visibility, and touch accuracy under representative glare.
- Physical measurement: ambient lux where available, viewing angle, panel
  brightness setting, and mounting angle.
- Reviewer decision: accept, add mitigation, or reject.
- Open issue: record glare zones and unreadable content.
- Retest requirement: required after panel, coating, palette, mounting, or scale
  changes.

This review does not establish automotive sunlight-readability certification.
