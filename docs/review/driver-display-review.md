# Driver display review

- [ ] Speed, RPM, gear, freshness, and factory-instrument guidance stay visible.
- [ ] Daily, Performance, and Track hierarchy is understandable.
- [ ] Advisory, caution, critical, multiple, stale, and disconnected states are distinct without colour alone.
- [ ] Warning overlay does not cover critical information and has correct z-order.
- [ ] Day, night, high contrast, reduced motion, minimum scale, and maximum scale are readable.
- [ ] Expanded and RTL pseudo-locales do not displace critical values.
- [ ] No interaction implies control of the vehicle or resolution of a warning.
- [ ] Findings include screenshot, profile, locale, theme, scenario, and commit.

Desktop review is not physical usability or automotive safety validation.
